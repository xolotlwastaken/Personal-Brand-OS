# Starting brief — refine through brand discovery

This is the user's starting brief. It is not a completed positioning study.

Focus: AI automation, entrepreneurship, building a personal brand with AI, helping creators save time and improve content, and practical tutorials showing useful workflows.

The user is documenting a **90-day challenge to use AI to grow an Instagram personal brand**, mainly through daily Reels about what they build, test, learn and automate. Weekly long-form YouTube tutorials are also planned. No challenge start date or current day has been provided.

Audience: ambitious young creators and entrepreneurs who want brands or side projects and may have little technical experience.

Desired content: practical, clear, authentic, personal, slightly contrarian when appropriate, useful to nontechnical people, grounded in actual workflows and results. Avoid generic polished AI language. Preserve the user's personality and experiences.

Missing: background, voice samples, real experiments/results, handle, actual current workflow, distinctive opinions, constraints and approved positioning. Do not invent any of these.

Brand Manager owns refinements. Propose major changes for human approval. Read [[brand/creator-profile]], [[brand/strategy]], [[brand/voice]], [[brand/audience]] and [[brand/content-pillars]].
